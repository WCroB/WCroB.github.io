Welcome to Wilson Crochet's online portal.

The purpose of this site is to have a platform to present my projects, my career and myself; independently from my CV, résumé or LinkedIn page.

About me

I build automated manufacturing systems, both in the physical and cyber spaces.

From the mechanical design to its commissionning on-site; from developing a PLC program to ensuring proper communication with desired systems, I aim to create machines that execute their tasks flawlessly and lastingly.

Values

I have always been fascinated by creation. I used to build towers out of Kapla planks before I was introduced to Minecraft, a game that shaped the person I am today. My drive to create finds its outlet in engineering and factories, where I can focus my energy on solving real-world problems and making a meaningful impact on the lives of many.

Boundlessly imaginative by nature, I thrive in environments where ideas can be openly discussed and successfully brought to life.

Curiosity drives me, but pragmatism keeps me grounded. I enjoy ambitious ideas, yet I believe their true value lies in their ability to solve real problems. Honesty and transparency are equally important to me: I value clear communication, candid feedback, and the willingness to admit when one does not know something. They build trust, foster better collaboration, and ultimately lead to better outcomes.
